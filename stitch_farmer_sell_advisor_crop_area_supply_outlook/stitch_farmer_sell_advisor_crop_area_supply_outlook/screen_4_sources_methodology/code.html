Mathematical Rigor & Constraints

Assumptions & Model Parameters

Assumption 1 Phenology

Sowing-to-Harvest Delay

90 – 105 days for Kharif red onion

Maturity curves track standard bulb sizing after transplanting. Extreme rainfall anomalies shift expected arrivals into mandis by +7 to +14 calendar days.

Assumption 2 Yield Baseline

District Productivity Norm

12.5 tonnes / hectare

Derived directly from ICAR-Directorate of Onion and Garlic Research (ICAR-DOGR) 5-year rolling yield data across southern plateau agro-climatic zones.

Limit 3 Safety Threshold

Crowdsource Statistical Gate

< 30 Farmers or < 5% Acreage Coverage

If village reporting falls below this threshold, the algorithm classifies data as unstable and flags the banner: “Too few reports to trust”.

Principle 4 Econometric Engine

Price Elasticity Modeling

Historical APMC arrival-price elasticity

Price forecasts are derived from historical arrival elasticities at regional APMCs. Individual farm declarations never directly influence price predictions.
