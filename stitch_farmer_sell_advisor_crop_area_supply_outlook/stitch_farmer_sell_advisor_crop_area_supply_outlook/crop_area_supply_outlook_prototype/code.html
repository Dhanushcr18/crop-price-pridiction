Transparent Methodology & Data Sources

Every number in Farmer Sell Advisor carries provenance. Here is how signals are constructed.

1. Strict Separation of Farmer & Official Data

Non-negotiable architectural boundary

We never extrapolate individual farmer reports into state or national aggregates. Crowd entries are strictly used for local district verification if and only if $\ge 30$ farmers or $\ge 5\%$ of district area is recorded. State and national acreages are sourced exclusively from published Directorate of Economics & Statistics bulletins.

2. Why Prices are Displayed as Rounded Ranges

Honesty by design principle

Agriculture markets possess natural volatility from weather variations, transport costs, and storage losses. Providing a single point price (e.g., ₹3,412/q) creates false precision. We round prices to the nearest ₹10 and provide high, mid, and low probability bounds with an explicit confidence rating.

3. Explicit Agricultural Assumptions (Labelled)

Clear assumption disclosure

Assumption 1 (Duration): Kharif onion nursery transplantation to harvest is modelled as 90 to 105 days.
Assumption 2 (Post-harvest Loss): Normal curing and transport shrinkage is pegged at 12.5% for early red varieties.
Assumption 3 (Productivity): Normal yield for irrigated Karnataka onion is pegged at 16.2 MT/hectare.
